About Me

Hello, I'm Miguel Aguirre Jr, a fourth year graduate student at the University of California, Riverside working under prinicpal investigator Dr. Boerge Hemmerling. His group is interested in probing and manipulating atoms and molecules by exploiting their quantum nature to resonant electromagnetic radiation. With our current experimental setup, we have the ability to create a buffer gas beam of cold atoms or molecules and resolve electronic transitions with absolute frequencies. We are also at the early stages of setting up our laser cooling and trapping system using chirped slowing in combination with a Magneto Optical Trap.
